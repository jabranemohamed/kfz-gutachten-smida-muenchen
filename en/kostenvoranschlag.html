Short Assessment & Repair Cost Estimate

Always here for you

What is a repair cost estimate?

A repair cost estimate is the simple and cost-effective way to assess vehicle damage. It serves as a non-binding preliminary calculation of the repair costs and works like a quote you can use for orientation.

Short assessment

More than just numbers

The short assessment goes well beyond a simple repair cost estimate. It takes important factors into account, such as:

  • The vehicle's roadworthiness
  • Standard and optional equipment
  • Potential repair risks

Why a short assessment

  • To assess major repairs
  • To assess medium-sized damage
  • You want clarity for your insurer, repair shop or a sale
