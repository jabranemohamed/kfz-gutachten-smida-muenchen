Third-Party Liability Report

Always here for you

Had an accident? We're by your side.

If you're involved in an accident that wasn't your fault, an independent third-party liability expert report is essential for enforcing your claims. As experienced motor vehicle assessors, we prepare legally binding expert reports that precisely assess the damage to your vehicle.

Benefits

What happens after the accident

Report the damage

Report the accident to your insurer right away – even if you're not at fault. This safeguards your claims and avoids misunderstandings.

Call us now

Give us a call and we'll take care of a thorough damage assessment. On request, we come straight to you on site and prepare a legally sound expert report.

Legal support

On request, our partner lawyers handle all correspondence with the other party's insurer. You can relax and leave the work to us.

Fast repair

We recommend the best repair route and coordinate everything with the repair shop of your choice. That gets your vehicle back on the road quickly.

Mobile again

With us at your side, the claims settlement is stress-free. We take care of everything – so you're back on the road in no time.
